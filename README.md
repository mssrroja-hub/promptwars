# MentiNous AI 🧠

**Your day, your priorities, your plan.**

MentiNous AI is a voice-first AI productivity companion designed for students, working professionals, developers, freelancers, and entrepreneurs. It helps users organize tasks, build realistic schedules, manage deadlines, and adapt when plans change.

**Core principle: The AI proposes. The user decides.**

## ✨ Features

- **Voice-First AI Planning:** Describe tasks and commitments using voice or text and receive a proposed daily schedule.
- **Adaptive Scheduling:** Adjust your plan when tasks take longer, priorities change, or new commitments appear.
- **Task Management:** Create, complete, postpone, and reschedule tasks while tracking daily progress.
- **Missed-Task Notifications:** Receive reminders and choose to complete, reschedule, request more time, or dismiss a reminder.
- **Calendar:** Organize tasks, events, and deadlines in calendar views.
- **MentiExercise:** Take an optional mental break with four interactive games:
  - **Matrix Master** — visual patterns and matrix reasoning.
  - **Memory Shift** — sequence and memory challenges.
  - **NeuroSprint** — numerical and logical challenges.
  - **Minty's Star Dash** — reflex and obstacle-avoidance gameplay.
- **Daily Review:** Review completed, unfinished, and rescheduled tasks without guilt or judgment.
- **Insights:** Explore task completion and planning trends using real data.
- **Dynamic Authentication:** Personalized accounts, login, registration, and user-specific data.
- **Privacy Controls:** Manage notifications and preferences without covert device monitoring.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

MentiNous AI uses a modern, premium, gender-neutral design intended to appeal to both students and professionals. The interface emphasizes clean typography, thoughtful spacing, subtle animations, and a balanced palette.

The optional companion, Minty, should never be required to use the application. Users should be able to hide the mascot while keeping task notifications enabled.

## 🧭 How It Works

1. Tell MentiNous what you need to accomplish.
2. Review the tasks and commitments it understood.
3. Approve or adjust the proposed schedule.
4. Track progress as you complete tasks.
5. Request changes whenever your day changes.
6. Review your progress and plan what comes next.
