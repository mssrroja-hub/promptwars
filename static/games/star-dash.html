How to Play Minty's Star Dash

  • Steer your runner ship using Arrow Keys (Left/Right), A/D keys, or by moving your mouse across the canvas.
  • Collect bright gold stars to earn 100 points each.
  • Dodge cosmic asteroids. Hitting an obstacle costs 1 shield.
  • Shield orbs replenish your defensive barriers.
  • Never forced to exit when timer ends — practice as long as you want in casual mode!
